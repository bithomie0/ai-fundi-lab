Hakuna Matata: a first website that does the explaining.

A bilingual website for a daycare in Mettmann, Germany that ran for years without one — so parents now arrive already understanding the concept.

ClientHakuna Matata Daycare
TypeFirst-ever business website
LocationMettmann, Germany
Livehakunamatata.biz

The problem

The daycare had run successfully for years on reputation alone — but with no website, every prospective parent started from zero. The owner's concept, daily routines, and values had to be explained from scratch in every conversation, and families comparing childcare options online simply never found her.

The build

  • Concept-first structure — the site is built around what parents actually want to know before choosing childcare: the philosophy, the routines, the values, the practicalities.
  • Bilingual — German and English, reflecting the daycare's international families.
  • Built with AI tools — professional design and copy at a price a single-owner daycare can afford, delivered without the owner needing any technical involvement.

"Parents now come to me already understanding how I work — and that makes choosing us much easier for them."

— Owner, Hakuna Matata Daycare

The result

The daycare's first-ever web presence now does the first conversation for her. Instead of explaining the basics on every phone call, the owner talks to parents who already know the concept, the routines, and the values — and are already halfway to a yes.
