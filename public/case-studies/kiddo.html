Kiddo: documentation that writes itself.

A voice-first AI platform that turns a childcare provider's spoken observations into structured developmental records, portfolios, and parent reports.

ProjectKiddo
TypeAI SaaS product
MarketGerman childcare (Kindertagespflege)
Livekiddo.now

The problem

German childcare providers are legally required to document each child's development — observations, portfolios, reports for parents. In practice that means educators spend their evenings typing up notes they scribbled between diaper changes. The documentation matters; the paperwork steals time from the children.

The build

Kiddo flips the workflow: the educator simply speaks. The platform does the rest.

  • Voice-first capture — record an observation in natural speech; AI transcribes it on the spot.
  • Automatic categorization — observations are sorted into development domains (motor, cognitive, social-emotional, language) using established German educational frameworks, including the Leuven Model.
  • Instant output — shareable portfolios and parent reports generated from the accumulated observations.
  • Daily routine logging — meals, naps, and diaper changes tracked in seconds.
  • Bilingual by design — full German and English interface with a one-tap toggle.
  • Built for trust — GDPR-compliant, with data stored in the EU (Frankfurt).

The product also got a complete brand identity: a warm, playful design system ("delightful, not childish") with a custom logo that reads as both a voice waveform and a smile.

The result

Kiddo is live in beta at kiddo.now, free for early testers, with German childcare providers documenting hands-free. It's also proof of the thesis this whole studio runs on: with today's AI tools, a small team can design, build, and ship a real software product — not in years, but in weeks.
